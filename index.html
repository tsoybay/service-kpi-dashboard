About these numbers

How is each number calculated?
Cars per working day
Repair orders completed, divided by the number of days that had at least one completion. Days when the shop was closed do not pull the average down.
Turnaround
Completed minus Opened, in calendar hours, so nights and weekends count. The median is the middle value, which a few very long jobs do not distort.
Share of orders
A technician's repair orders as a share of all the repair orders shown.

This version needs only five columns, so it does not measure on-time rate, first-time fixes or technician efficiency.

Data quality

The sample data is fictional and generated by code with a fixed seed. It does not describe any real shop, technician or customer. For information only.
